How to apply

From search to submission

Step 1

Check your country is eligible

Each card says which African countries can apply. Some programs publish a new country list every year.

Step 2

Gather your documents

International passport, transcripts, degree certificate, CV, IELTS/TOEFL (or an English-medium letter), and 2–3 references.

Step 3

Write your essays early

Personal statement, study plan or research proposal. Give yourself at least two months.

Step 4

Apply on the official site

Use the Apply button on each card. Every link goes to the scholarship's own website.

Funding types. Fully funded covers tuition, living costs and usually flights. Partially funded covers part of the cost, so you pay the rest. Tuition-free / waiver removes or cuts tuition, but you pay your own living costs.

Dates. Deadlines change every year. The months shown are when applications usually open. Confirm on the official page before you apply.

Avoid scams. No real scholarship asks you to pay an agent to be "selected". Apply only through the official links.
